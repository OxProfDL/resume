# Resume Review Guide

Use this checklist when reviewing a resume.

## 1) Grammar checks

- Ensure every bullet starts with a strong action verb.
- Keep verb tense consistent:
  - Past tense for completed roles
  - Present tense only for current role responsibilities
- Remove sentence fragments that do not add measurable value.
- Keep punctuation style consistent across bullets (either all with periods or all without).
- Replace vague claims with specific outcomes.

## 2) Spell-check checks

- Check for typos in:
  - Job titles
  - Company names
  - Technical skills
  - Certifications
- Verify capitalization consistency for technologies (for example: GitHub, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL).
- Confirm dates, locations, and proper nouns are spelled correctly.

## 3) Further improvement suggestions

- Prioritize measurable achievements over task descriptions.
- Keep bullets concise and avoid repeating similar points.
- Tailor skill keywords to the target role description.
- Remove outdated or low-signal content.
- Ensure formatting and section order support a quick 10-15 second scan.
